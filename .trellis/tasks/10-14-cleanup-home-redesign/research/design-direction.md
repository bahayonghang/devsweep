## Direction dimensions

Audience: developers weighing recoverable capacity against rebuild/reinstall cost.
Aesthetic: neutral utility, 8px controls, opaque surface steps, green accents.
Signature: original beaver and aligned truthful capacities.
Constraints: existing React/Tauri/plain CSS/Base UI, no new production dependency, offline assets, accessibility, preserved appearance choices.
Micro-interaction: brief pointer press feedback, immediate keyboard behavior, no perpetual decoration.
