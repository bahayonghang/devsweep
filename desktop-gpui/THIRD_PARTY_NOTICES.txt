Made with Natural Earth (Tom Patterson, Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso and contributors).
Source: https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_raster/NE1_LR_LC_SR_W.zip

Natural Earth raster/vector map data are public domain, including redistribution

archive's VERSION.txt says 2.0.0. Exact source identity is pinned below.
Archive SHA-256: 8e30c15d49bc73223a36eaeffc07fd49998e658e373a5498f276f5d26bfd1d0a

Conversion (offline, 2026-10-13, Pillow 12.3.0): Lanczos 16200x8100 RGB to a
1024x512 PNG with no geographic or color edits, then a 512x512 RGBA sphere still
with 20-degree longitude, bilinear sampling and fixed lighting. The conversion
script shipped with the former Tauri desktop app, which was removed on
2026-11-14; the still is kept byte-identical.
